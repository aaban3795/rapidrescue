          How it works

          1. Create an account

            Sign up as a citizen, hospital or ambulance with your name, email and a password.

          2. Add emergency contacts

            Save the people who should be told where you are.

          3. Press SOS

            The browser asks to use your location. Choose Allow.

          4. Hospital and contacts get your location

            Hospitals see your name, phone, blood group and exact location, and the closest hospital to you gets priority. Your contacts get an email with a Google Maps link.

          5. Hospital sends an ambulance

            The hospital login forwards the case to an ambulance login.

          6. Ambulance goes and rates urgency

            The ambulance team opens directions to the citizen and marks the case High, Medium or Low urgency.

          7. Check your History

            Every login type can open History to see past cases and what happened at each step.

          First aid after an accident

          General guidance only, not training or medical advice. Call emergency services first.

          Stay safe first

          • Switch on hazard lights and move away from traffic.
          • Keep away from a smoking, leaking or burning vehicle.
          • Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.

          Severe bleeding

          • Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and keep pressing.
          • Add more cloth on top rather than removing soaked cloth.
          • Keep the person lying down and warm.

          Not breathing or unresponsive

          • Call emergency services and shout for help.
          • If trained, start CPR: 30 chest compressions, then 2 breaths.
          • Use an AED if one is available.

          Possible head or spine injury

          • Keep the head, neck and back still.
          • Do not remove a rider's helmet unless the airway is blocked.
          • Keep watching their breathing until help arrives.

          About RapidRescue

          RapidRescue is a smart accident emergency response project. Its first feature makes it quick to share your location when something goes wrong on the road.

          Privacy

          • Location is read only when you press SOS and is never tracked in the background.
          • Your account, contacts, profile and SOS cases are stored only in this browser on this device. A live version for several devices needs a server.
          • Passwords are stored as a salted hash, never as plain text.

          Limits

          RapidRescue depends on GPS, internet and email delivery. It is an extra safety aid, not a replacement for calling emergency services.

          RapidRescue shares your location with your contacts and with the hospitals and ambulance teams registered here. It does not contact official emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number first.
